Preliminary data. Do not cite. These figures come from pipeline outputs dated April 2026, built before several corrections were applied. Totals and source shares will change when the tables are regenerated.
What is known to be out of date
  • Land-use attribution has not been re-run since April; a pasture-to-livestock step was later found to fail when run end to end.
  • Livestock residual fixes from July 2026 (camel, duck, turkey, horse, mule, ass, llama mapped to real commodities) are not reflected.
  • Commodity-mapping bugs in the trade layer found in September 2026 (including sugar mapped to fish) affect the consumption side and the trade flows.
  • Food groups are assigned by keyword and are wrong for about 16% of emissions: cattle and buffalo milk are counted under Beef & Buffalo, goat milk under Cereals, and pears and peaches under Pulses. Dairy & Eggs is understated as a result.
  • Food transport is a bottom-up estimate (about 175% of the FAOSTAT figure), on a different accounting basis from the other sources.
